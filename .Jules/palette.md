## 2026-08-09 - Full-word Accessibility Labels for Badges
**Learning:** Abbreviated UI elements like 'AD' badges require full-word `accessibilityLabel`s (e.g., 'Advertisement') to ensure screen readers pronounce them correctly, rather than reading them as individual letters or unrelated words.
**Action:** Always provide full-word, localized `accessibilityLabel`s for abbreviated badges or UI elements.
