## 2024-05-18 - Full Word Accessibility Labels for Abbreviations
**Learning:** Expanding abbreviations like "AD" to their full words (e.g., "Advertisement") via `accessibilityLabel` ensures that VoiceOver pronounces them correctly instead of spelling them out (like "A D") or mispronouncing them. This is a critical pattern for ad tags across all components.
**Action:** Always provide full-word `accessibilityLabel` strings for abbreviated UI elements or badges to ensure proper screen reader pronunciation.
