## 2024-05-20 - VoiceOver and Abbreviations
**Learning:** Short abbreviated strings like "AD" on badges are often mispronounced by VoiceOver as "add" or spelled out awkwardly "A-D", reducing the professional feel and clear intent for screen reader users. Always use the full word `accessibilityLabel` like "Advertisement" for better clarity.
**Action:** Always provide full-word `accessibilityLabel` strings for abbreviated UI elements or badges to ensure proper screen reader pronunciation.
