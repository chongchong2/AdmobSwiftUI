## 2026-05-24 - VoiceOver pronunciation for Abbreviations
**Learning:** Screen readers like VoiceOver may incorrectly pronounce short, capitalized abbreviations (e.g., reading 'AD' as the word 'add').
**Action:** Always expand abbreviations using `accessibilityLabel` with full words (e.g., 'Advertisement') to ensure proper pronunciation and context for visually impaired users.
