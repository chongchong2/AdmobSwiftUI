## 2026-05-07 - VoiceOver pronunciation for acronym badges
**Learning:** Screen readers often mispronounce acronyms or short UI badges (like 'AD' for Advertisement) by either reading them as words or spelling them out incorrectly.
**Action:** Always provide a fully expanded `accessibilityLabel` (e.g., 'Advertisement') for short UI badges to ensure precise VoiceOver pronunciation.
