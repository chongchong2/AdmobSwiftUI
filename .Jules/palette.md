## 2024-05-08 - Screen Reader Pronunciation for Abbreviations
**Learning:** VoiceOver and other screen readers often mispronounce short abbreviations (like "AD" for advertisement) or read them as individual letters.
**Action:** Always provide full-word `accessibilityLabel` strings for abbreviated UI elements or badges (e.g., use 'Advertisement' for 'AD' badges) to ensure proper screen reader pronunciation.
