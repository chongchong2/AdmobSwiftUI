## 2024-05-24 - Screen Reader Pronunciation of Abbreviations
**Learning:** Abbreviations like "AD" for advertisement are often read phonetically or spelled out (e.g., "A D") by VoiceOver, confusing users. Using full words for accessibility labels ensures accurate context.
**Action:** Always provide full-word, localized `accessibilityLabel` strings (e.g., "Advertisement") for abbreviated badges or UI elements to ensure proper screen reader pronunciation.
