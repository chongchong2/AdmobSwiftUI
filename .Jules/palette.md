## 2024-05-24 - Added VoiceOver pronouncation for Ad badges
**Learning:** Short abbreviations like "AD" are often spelled out letter-by-letter or mispronounced by screen readers like VoiceOver.
**Action:** Always provide a full-word `accessibilityLabel` (e.g., "Advertisement") for abbreviations to ensure proper screen reader pronunciation.
